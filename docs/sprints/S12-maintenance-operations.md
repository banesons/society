# S12 — Maintenance Operations

## Objective
Operate daily maintenance accounting: bill generation, dues tracking, payment recording, ledger management, and member statements.

## Scope
### Backend
- Bill generation batch/manual triggers based on maintenance configuration.
- Unit ledger tracking for debits (bills), credits (payments), and outstanding balances.
- Payment recording (cash, bank transfer, digital payment reference) and receipt generation.
- Unit statement generation and dues calculation endpoints.

### Frontend
- Billing operations dashboard for Society Admins and Treasurers.
- Manual payment entry and offline collection logging interface.
- Resident dues screen, detailed bill breakdown, and downloadable payment receipts.
- Unit ledger statement view for residents and administrators.

## Deliverables
- Automated and manual bill generation workflows.
