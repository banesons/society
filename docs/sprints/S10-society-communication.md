# S10 — Society Communication

## Objective
Enable society-wide and audience-scoped communication through official notices, announcements, and baseline notification events.

## Scope
### Backend
- Notice creation, publication, updating, and expiry endpoints.
- Audience scoping logic (entire society, specific building/wing, owners only, tenants only).
- Read acknowledgment tracking for important notices.
- Basic communication event generation for downstream notification consumers.

### Frontend
- Notice composer and publishing management console for Society Admins.
- Digital notice board and announcement feed for residents.
- Audience filtering and scheduling controls.
- Read receipt tracking and acknowledgment view for administrators.

## Deliverables
- Notice publishing and audience targeting system.
- Resident digital notice board with categorized announcements.
- Acknowledgment tracking for mandatory society communications.
